Check 3: Problem 9 usability feature, sold-out size → in-stock alternatives (B2)

Why this feature: a sold-out size is the moment a shopper is most likely to leave. find_alternatives turns that "no" into in-stock options in their size, so it has the most direct effect on sales.

Shopper picked size XL on the Baseball Left Chest Crewneck page and asked: "Is this available in XL?"
Bulldog Bot replied: "Sorry, XL is sold out in Baseball Left Chest Crewneck; S, M, L and XXL are available. The options on the page are available in XL: Yale Sports Crewneck Baseball shares the baseball theme and crewneck style, Tri Blend Sports Baseball T Shirt shares the baseball theme, and Hype And Vice Yale University Offside Crewneck is another crewneck in navy and white."
